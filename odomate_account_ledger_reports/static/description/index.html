Accounting Audit Reports

General Ledger · Partner Ledger · Aged Balance · Tax Report · Journals Audit · Journal Entry

The six audit printouts that accountants and auditors ask for, as clean PDF documents, straight from Odoo Community. Every report is built on one shared filter — company, period, journals, posted or all entries — so the team learns a single dialog and gets six documents out of it. Nothing is written back to your books: the module reads journal items through the ORM and adds no field to any standard model.

Key Features

Correct opening balances

Balance-sheet accounts open with everything booked before the start date; income, expense and Current Year Earnings accounts open with the fiscal year to date only — exactly as an auditor expects.

Ageing measured as of a date

The Aged Partner Balance rebuilds each item's residual from the reconciliation history, so a payment posted after the ageing date does not silently shrink last month's report.

Honest tax signs

The Tax Report negates the Sales section instead of taking absolute values, so credit notes genuinely reduce both the net and the tax figures.

One filter, six reports

Company, Start Date, End Date, Journals and Posted / All Entries behave identically everywhere, and each dialog adds only what it really needs — accounts, partners, analytic accounts, period length, sort order.

Multi-company and multi-currency aware

Each report is pinned to the chosen company and printed in that company's currency. An optional Currency column shows an item's own foreign amount where it differs; nothing is ever converted behind your back.

Two journal-item screens

Journal Items by Account and Journal Items by Partner ship as ready-made list / pivot / graph views over account.move.line for when you need a spreadsheet instead of a PDF.

How it works

  1. Open Accounting → Reporting and pick a report: Audit Reports holds the General Ledger and the Journals Audit, Partner Reports holds the Partner Ledger and the three ageing reports, Taxes holds the Tax Report.
  2. Set the period, choose journals if you need to narrow things down, and leave Entries on Posted Entries Only for an audit-grade document.
  3. Add the report-specific options — opening balance and account display for the General Ledger, account type and settled items for the Partner Ledger, as-of date and period length for the ageing.
  4. Press Print. The dialog validates your filter first and explains in plain words if something is off — a start date after the end date, a journal from another company, a company you are not allowed to work in.
  5. Need a single entry on paper? Select entries in Journal Entries and use Print → Journal Entry. Need a customer statement? Select contacts and use Print → Partner Ledger.

Security

Only the three standard accounting groups — Billing Administrator, Accountant and read-only accounting access — see the menus and may print. The module creates no group of its own, grants nothing to anyone else, writes nothing outside its own wizard records and uses no sudo(): every figure you see is a figure you are allowed to see.
