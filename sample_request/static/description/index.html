Product Sample Requests

Sample approvals, automatic follow-up and sample-to-order conversion reporting

Sales reps request product samples for their customers right in Odoo. Sales managers approve or reject them and mark approved samples as sent. Odoo then reminds the rep to follow up 14 days later, records whether the customer ordered, and shows each rep's conversion rate in a pivot report.

Key Features

Requests & approvals

  • Sample requests (sample.request) with Customer, Sales Rep, Reason and a Products tab with product and quantity lines
  • Automatic references such as SR/2026/0001 and a full status history in the chatter
  • Draft → Submitted → Approved → Sent → Followed Up, plus Rejected and Cancelled
  • Reject dialog with a required Rejection Reason, and Decided By recorded on approval or rejection
  • Reps see only their own requests and cannot edit them after submitting. Managers see all requests

Follow-up & analytics

  • Mark as Sent sets the Sent Date and schedules a "Follow up on sample request" to-do for the rep, due 14 days later
  • Log Outcome dialog: Ordered, Still Thinking or Not Interested, with an optional link to a Sales Order
  • Logging the outcome marks the follow-up to-do as done automatically
  • Pivot report by Sales Rep and Status with Count, Converted and Conversion Rate (%)
  • Two roles: Sample Request / User and Sample Request / Manager

How it works

A rep opens Sample Requests → My Requests, creates a request for a customer, adds the products and clicks Submit. A manager reviews it under Approvals and clicks Approve or Reject. After shipping, the manager clicks Mark as Sent, and the rep gets a to-do due 14 days after the Sent Date. When the rep has talked to the customer, they click Log Outcome. Reporting opens with the "Sent or Followed Up" filter on, so Conversion Rate (%) = ordered ÷ (sent + followed up). For example, 3 orders from 10 samples gives 30%.
