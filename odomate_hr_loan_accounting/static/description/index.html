Employee Loans — Accounting Entries

Every loan movement, posted to the ledger. Automatically, once, and only once.

odomate_hr_loan runs the employee loan workflow and stores every figure it needs. This module is its accounting half: it posts a disbursement entry when a loan is approved, one recovery entry per instalment as payroll recovers it, and a settlement entry when a loan is settled early. Amounts are read off the records that already hold them — never recalculated — so a deferred or hand-adjusted instalment posts exactly what it shows.

Key Features

Three entries, no clicks

Approve a loan and the disbursement entry is posted. Confirm a payslip and the instalment's entry is posted, dated the payroll period's end date. Settle early and the closing entry is posted on the settlement date you typed.

Impossible to double-post

Each instalment carries its own account_move_id. Once it is set, that row is done for ever — re-confirming or recomputing a payslip cannot produce a second entry.

Refuses before it breaks

A missing journal, a journal with no default account, a missing interest account or an employee with no contact stops approval with a message naming the problem — the loan stays submitted rather than half-processed.

Configured once, per company

A loan journal, a loan receivable account and an interest income account on the Accounting settings page. They become the defaults on every new loan, and every loan can override them.

No accounting rights needed

HR and payroll officers trigger the postings without holding accounting roles. Elevated rights are scoped to the journal-entry create-and-post pair alone — never to the loan, the instalment or the employee.

Nothing is ever rewritten

The module never reverses, unlinks or edits a posted entry. Reopening a loan leaves its ledger history exactly as it is.

How it works

A 12 000.00 loan over 4 monthly instalments at 10 % interest stores principal 3 000.00 and interest 300.00 per instalment.

  • On approval — debit Loans to Employees 12 000.00, credit the journal's default account 12 000.00, with the employee as partner.
  • On each payslip — debit the counterpart 3 300.00, credit Loans to Employees 3 000.00 and Interest Income 300.00. The interest line is omitted entirely on interest-free loans.
  • On early settlement — debit the counterpart with the outstanding balance and credit the principal and interest still open.

Once the loan finishes — on schedule or settled early — its contribution to the receivable account is exactly zero. The Journal Entries smart button on the loan opens everything posted for it.
