=================
Employee Transfer
=================

Records, approves and applies dated employee moves — department, job position,
work location, manager and company — as **new** ``hr.version`` history entries.
Instead of overwriting the department on an employee form and losing the
previous value, HR creates an ``odomate.hr.transfer`` record with an effective
date, sends it for approval, and lets an HR Manager approve it. When the
effective date arrives the transfer is applied and a new dated version is added
to the employee's history. The module never overwrites the employee's existing
version, never duplicates the employee record and never archives one.

Installation
============

To install this module, you need to:

#. Clone or download the module into your Odoo addons path.
#. Update the apps list (Apps → Update Apps List).
#. Search for "Employee Transfer" and click Install.

The module depends on ``hr`` and ``mail``, both of which Odoo installs
automatically if they are missing.

Configuration
=============

No configuration is required. Installation creates:

#. The sequence ``odomate.hr.transfer`` (references ``TRF/00001``, ``TRF/00002`` …).
#. The daily scheduled action **Employee Transfer: Apply Due Transfers**, which
   applies approved transfers whose effective date has arrived. Disable it under
   Settings → Technical → Automation → Scheduled Actions if transfers should only
   ever be applied by hand.

Access is based on the standard Employees groups: ``hr.group_hr_user`` (Officer)
can create and edit transfers, ``hr.group_hr_manager`` (Administrator) can also
approve and delete them.

Usage
=====

#. Navigate to **Employees → Transfers**, or use the **Transfers** smart button
   on any employee form.
#. Click **New**, pick the **Employee** and the **Effective Date**, then fill one
   or more of **To Department**, **To Job Position**, **To Work Location**,
   **To Manager** and **To Company**. The left-hand column shows the current
   values (**From Department**, **From Job Position** …); an empty field on the
   right means "no change".
#. Click **Send for Approval**. HR Managers with access to the companies involved
   get a *To Do* activity due on the effective date.
#. An HR Manager clicks **Approve**. For a cross-company move the target company
   must be one of the approver's allowed companies — this is enforced inside
   ``action_approve``, not only by hiding the button.
#. Once the effective date has arrived, the **Apply now** button appears (or the
   scheduled action fires overnight). Applying creates a new ``hr.version`` dated
   on the effective date with the new department, job position and work location,
   and writes the new manager and company onto the employee record.

**Refuse** (with a mandatory reason, entered in the
``odomate.hr.transfer.refuse`` wizard) and **Cancel** end the transfer without
writing anything at all to the employee record.

For the full step-by-step guide, see ``doc/USER_GUIDE.md``
(Ukrainian: ``doc/USER_GUIDE.uk.md``).

Known limitations
=================

* Applying is final. There is no un-apply, no reset to draft and no undo —
  reversing a move means recording another transfer back to the previous values.
* Manager (``parent_id``) and company (``company_id``) are **not** part of the
  dated version history, because Odoo does not version those two fields. The
  module therefore cannot answer "who was this employee's manager on date X".
  Only department, job position and work location get real dated history in
  ``hr.version``.
* The effective date must be strictly later than the employee's most recent
  ``hr.version`` date, so a move cannot be back-dated before the last version.
* No pay or contract changes: wage, contract type and working schedule are
  carried over unchanged from the previous version.
* No bulk transfers, no org-chart preview, no reporting-loop check.
* No transfer letters, no PDF reports and no email to the employee — the only
  notification is the internal *To Do* activity for HR Managers.
* No cross-company "Send / Receive" handshake and no second approval level.

See ``doc/USER_GUIDE.md`` section "Limitations" for the full list.
