Employee Transfer

Dated employee moves recorded as real version history — never as an overwrite

An employee changes department on 1 October. In standard Odoo you open the employee form, overwrite the department, and the previous value is gone. This module replaces that with a small auditable record — model odomate.hr.transfer — carrying an effective date, an approval step and a chatter trail. When the transfer is applied it creates a new hr.version entry dated on the effective date. It never overwrites the existing version, never duplicates the employee record and never archives one. Built for HR Officers and HR Managers using the standard Employees app.

Key Features

Recording the move

  • Five dimensions in one record: To Department, To Job Position, To Work Location, To Manager, To Company.
  • Two-column before/after layout — From Department next to To Department, and so on. An empty row means "no change".
  • Automatic references from the odomate.hr.transfer sequence: TRF/00001, TRF/00002…
  • Two guardrails: at least one target must be set and must actually differ from the current value; the effective date must be strictly later than the employee's most recent hr.version.
  • Full mail.thread chatter with tracking on employee, effective date, status and every target field.

Approving and applying

  • Status flow draft → to_approve → approved → applied, with refused and cancelled as alternate endings.
  • Approve requires hr.group_hr_manager — checked inside action_approve, so scripts and API calls are blocked too, not just the button.
  • Cross-company moves can only be approved by someone whose allowed companies include the target company.
  • Apply now appears only once the effective date has arrived; a daily scheduled action, Employee Transfer: Apply Due Transfers, applies due transfers automatically.
  • Refuse (with a mandatory reason) and Cancel write nothing at all to the employee record.
  • Applied transfers become immutable: employee, dates, targets and status are locked.

How it works

Everything lives under Employees ▸ Transfers (visible to hr.group_hr_user), plus a Transfers smart button on every employee form. An HR Officer creates a transfer, picks the employee and the effective date, fills one or more "To …" fields, and clicks Send for Approval — which schedules a To Do activity for the HR Managers who cover the companies involved. An HR Manager clicks Approve. On the effective date, Apply now becomes available (or the nightly scheduled action fires first) and the move is committed.

Worked example. Marc Demo is in Sales; his latest version is dated 2026-06-11. TRF/00007 moves him to Customer Success effective 2026-10-01. After applying, his history holds two rows — 2026-06-11 → Sales and 2026-10-01 → Customer Success — and there is still exactly one Marc Demo in the employee list. Job position and work location carry over untouched because those fields were left empty. Asking "which department was he in on 15 August 2026?" correctly returns Sales.

Worth knowing up front. Applying is final — there is no undo, and reversing a move means recording another transfer. Department, job position and work location become real dated history in hr.version; manager (parent_id) and company (company_id) are written straight onto the employee because Odoo does not version those two fields, so this module cannot tell you who an employee's manager was on a past date. There are no pay or contract changes, no bulk transfers, no org-chart preview, no transfer letters or PDF reports, and no cross-company "Receive" handshake.
