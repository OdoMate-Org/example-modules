Office Equipment Tracker

Know who holds what, at a glance

Office Equipment Tracker gives IT and office managers a single, list-first view of every company device — laptops, phones, monitors and peripherals — including its current holder, its check-out and return dates, and condition notes. A simple status workflow moves each item between Available, Checked Out, In Repair and Retired.

Key Features

  • Current-holder tracking — link each item to the employee who currently holds it via the employee_id field.
  • Status workflow — Check Out, Return, Send to Repair and Retire buttons drive the state field.
  • Auto-filled dates — checkout date is set on check-out; return date is set on return.

  • Serial-number integrity — serial numbers stay unique across all active (non-retired) equipment.
  • Role-based access — Equipment Administrators manage everything; internal users see only the items assigned to them.
  • Multi-company ready — records are isolated per company through a record rule.

How It Works

Open Equipment > All Equipment and register a device. Set its current holder and click Check Out — the status becomes Checked Out and today's date is stored. When the device comes back, click Return; the holder is cleared and the return date recorded. Use Send to Repair or Retire at any point — both release the current holder while preserving the condition notes.

Full documentation is available in doc/USER_GUIDE.md inside the module.
