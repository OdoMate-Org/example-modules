OdoMate HR Resignation & Clearance

Offboard employees without losing the laptop

Track a resignation from the day notice is given, through manager and HR approval, across an exit clearance checklist, to the final release of the employee. The release stays blocked while the custody register still shows company property in that employee's hands, so nobody walks out with a projector still assigned to them.

The custody register is read, never written. It is re-read live at every release attempt rather than trusting the snapshot taken at HR approval - the two are allowed to disagree, and the live read always wins.

Key Features

  • Full state machine - draft, confirmed, manager approved, clearance, released, with refused and withdrawn as terminal branches.
  • Notice period from the contract - read from the employee's current hr.version, with a proposed last working day that stops mirroring the moment a human edits it.
  • Configurable checklist - per-company or globally shared clearance items, each routed to its own responsible user.
  • Optional manager approval - one checkbox per company in the Employees settings.
  • Two independent release blockers - outstanding company property and an incomplete checklist, each with its own distinctly worded message naming exactly what is missing.
  • Post-release immutability - enforced in write(), not merely read-only on the view.
  • Daily scheduled action - auto-releases records once the last working day has passed, running the same two checks as the button, with no elevated bypass.
  • Exit interview - through the standard survey.invite flow, with sent and answered indicators.

How it works

1. Record the notice. In Employees → Resignations, pick the employee and the notification date. The notice period is read from their current version and the proposed last working day is calculated: notice given on 8 September with a 30-day period proposes 8 October.

2. Approve. Confirm raises a To-Do activity on the employee's manager. Manager Approve hands over to HR; HR Approve builds the checklist from the active clearance items and raises one activity per responsible user.

3. Clear. Anything still on loan from the custody register is snapshotted into an extra blocked line naming each item, its handover reference and its due date. Responsible users clear their own line from their inbox - and see nothing else of the file.

4. Release. Once the checklist is complete and the live custody re-read comes back empty, one atomic step writes the departure date and reason on the employee, ends the current contract version, archives the employee and archives their login.

Security

No new security groups are created - the module reuses hr.group_hr_user and hr.group_hr_manager, so HR staff can use it the moment it installs. There is no resignation menu outside HR: the resigning employee's own manager reaches exactly one record through their approval activity, and every other internal user matches zero rows. Multi-company record rules are shipped on every model.
