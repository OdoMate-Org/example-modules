Product Kind — Manufacturing Classification

Classify once, keep history honest forever

A hierarchical, production-oriented Product Kind catalog that flows from products through sales into manufacturing. Confirmed sale order lines and manufacturing orders freeze a snapshot of the kind, so historical documents stay accurate even after a product is reclassified — while products and their bills of materials keep a live link to the current kind.

Key Features

Hierarchical catalog

A draggable multi-level tree (Hierarchy view) with List, Kanban and Form. Each kind carries a route type, a manufacturing mode, and engineering / team-planning flags.

Live link + frozen snapshot

Products and bills of materials always reflect the current kind. Confirmed sale order lines and manufacturing orders keep the kind they had at confirmation — never contradicting your history.

Snapshot immutability

Once frozen, a snapshot can only be changed by a Product Kind Manager — enforced at the model level, not just in the form, so direct API and import writes cannot bypass it.

Subtree smart buttons

From any kind, jump to the Products, BoMs, Sale Lines and Manufacturing Orders of its entire subtree, counted with child_of.

How it works

1. Build your Product Kind tree under Manufacturing ▸ Configuration ▸ Product Kinds.
2. Assign a kind to each product; derived route/mode attributes appear automatically.
3. Confirm a sale order — each line freezes its Product Kind snapshot.
4. Create a manufacturing order — it inherits the sale line's snapshot, or the product's current kind for manual orders.
5. Reclassify a product later — its BoM follows the new kind, while confirmed documents keep the old one.
