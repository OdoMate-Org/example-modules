Employee Loans

Lend, schedule, and recover from payroll — without a spreadsheet

Record a loan for an employee, build a fixed repayment schedule the moment it is approved, and let payroll do the rest. Every instalment falling due in a payslip period is added to that payslip automatically and marked as recovered once the payslip is confirmed. Deferrals, early settlement and a full audit trail are built in.

Key Features

A real repayment schedule

Approval builds one odomate.hr.loan.instalment record per instalment, with due date, principal, interest and total. Flat interest is spread evenly and rounding remainders land on the last row, so the figures always add up exactly.

Automatic payslip recovery

A shipped LOAN_REPAY salary rule sums every due instalment into a single deduction line. Recovery is keyed off payslip confirmation, not computation, so a payslip can be recomputed freely and confirming twice never recovers twice.

Defer and settle early

Push an instalment — and every later one — forward by a month, with the reason posted to the chatter. Or settle the loan early: remaining instalments are cancelled rather than deleted, so the schedule still reads as history.

Policy you control

Per company, decide whether an employee may hold more than one running loan, and cap the principal that may be approved. Both are enforced at approval time, against the principal only.

Locked down properly

Employees see only their own loans, enforced by record rules rather than hidden buttons — opening a colleague's loan by URL is refused server-side. Amounts and terms freeze once a loan is approved, for ORM and RPC writes too.

Ready for accounting

Instalments are stored as first-class records with real columns — never computed on the fly or packed into JSON — so a future accounting module can read principal, interest, state and payslip link directly.

How it works

  1. Capture — HR opens Employees → Loans → Loan Requests and records the employee, amount, purpose, instalment count, first instalment date and optional interest rate.
  2. Build the schedule — one click fills the instalment table. Editing the terms while still in draft clears the schedule, so a stale plan can never be submitted.
  3. Submit and approve — the one-loan-at-a-time rule and the maximum amount ceiling are checked at approval.
  4. Run payroll — instalments due in the period are added to the payslip and summed into a single Loan Repayment deduction.
  5. Confirm — the instalments become Recovered and the loan closes itself once nothing is left outstanding.

One-time setup

The module ships the Loan Repayment salary rule but deliberately does not attach it to a salary structure — it cannot know which one your company uses. Add it once under Payroll → Configuration → Salary Structures, and every payslip on that structure picks it up from then on.
