Sale Discount Approval

One discount for the whole order — with a manager gate for the big ones

Apply a single percent or fixed-amount discount that fans out to every sale order or invoice line, and route large discounts to a Sales Manager for approval before the order can be confirmed.

Key Features

  • Order-level and invoice-level discount, by Percent or fixed Amount.
  • The discount is spread evenly across every line automatically.
  • Informational Discount total on orders, invoices and printed documents.
  • Optional two-step approval: orders above a company discount limit move to Waiting Approval.

  • Approve / Reject buttons restricted to the Sales Manager group.
  • The discount is carried over automatically when the order is invoiced.
  • Negative rates act as a surcharge — no floor validation.
  • Discount row injected into quotation, invoice and portal documents.

Getting started

Enable Settings > Sales > Discounts (Discount per line) to reveal the discount fields, then turn on Sale Discount Approval in the same page and set your approval limit.
