OdoMate HR Overview Dashboard

Five numbers your HR team actually acts on - and the exact records behind each one

A read-only management overview for Odoo 19 Employees. It answers five questions on one screen - how many people do we have, who joined, who left, who is off today, and what is our turnover rate - and every figure is clickable, opening the precise list of records it was counted from. Below the tiles it ranks absence concentration by the Bradford factor and collects everything from the wider OdoMate HR suite that is waiting for an approval.

The module only reads. It creates no records of its own, grants no security group to anybody, and never uses sudo() - every figure is computed with the permissions of the person looking at it.

Key Features

  • Five figure tiles - Headcount, Joined, Left, Off today and Turnover, laid out on a responsive CSS grid.
  • Drill-down parity - the row count of every opened list equals the figure that opened it, because both are built from the same domain in the same server call.
  • Period selector - two plain date inputs, defaulting to the current calendar year. Headcount and Off today deliberately ignore it and say so, right on the tile.
  • Worked turnover arithmetic - the tile shows "2 departures / average headcount 121.5 (122 on 2026-01-01, 121 on 2026-12-31) = 1.6%", not a generic sentence.
  • Joiners and leavers - the odomate.hr.movement SQL view, one row per arrival and per departure, with list, graph, pivot and search views. Archived leavers included.
  • Absence concentration - the odomate.hr.absence.factor SQL view ranks employees by the Bradford factor (occurrences squared, multiplied by days) over approved, finished time off.
  • Cross-suite approvals - loans, salary advances, resignations, transfers and announcements are counted only when that module is installed and you may read it.
  • Multi-company by record rule - both analysis models ship a company rule, and every server-side query is scoped to self.env.companies.

How It Works

Open Employees > HR Overview. The component makes one call to odomate.hr.dashboard.get_overview() and renders the payload - no arithmetic happens in the browser. Change either date input and the call is repeated; the period-driven tiles relabel themselves with the dates you picked.

Tap a tile to open its records. Tap Joiners and leavers, Absence by employee or Headcount by department to open the plain, unfiltered analysis lists and narrow them yourself with the search panel - the same convention Odoo's own analysis views follow. Access is limited to hr.group_hr_user and hr.group_hr_manager; a plain internal user sees no menu and cannot reach the data by address.
