Employee Dependants & Identity Documents

Dependants, identity document expiry reminders and login-to-employee integrity for Odoo 19

Every HR team ends up keeping family details and passport expiry dates in a spreadsheet beside Odoo. This module brings them back into the employee file: a dependants list with exactly one enforced emergency contact, expiry dates and scans for the identification reference and the passport, and a daily reminder that fires once — on the day your configured lead time is reached — instead of nagging every morning.

It also closes two small gaps that cost HR time: a stored Joining Date computed from the employee's own version history, and an optional switch that creates the employee record automatically whenever a new internal login is added.

Key features

  • Dependants per employee — name, relationship, phone and date of birth on the HR-only Private Information page (odomate.hr.dependant).
  • Editable relationship master list — Spouse, Father, Mother, Son and Daughter ship with the module; add Guardian or anything else from Employees → Configuration (odomate.hr.relationship).
  • One emergency contact, guaranteed — enforced in create and write and backed by a partial unique index, so imports and API calls obey the same rule as the form.
  • Automatic Spouse dependant — filled in from spouse_complete_name and spouse_birthdate, idempotent so re-saving never duplicates it.

  • Identity document expiry — identification_expiry_date plus multi-file scan attachments for both the identification reference and the passport.
  • Edge-triggered daily reminder — an ir.cron that matches today + lead time exactly, so nobody is warned twice, plus two "send now" buttons.
  • Editable e-mail templates — wording and translations go through the standard mail.template mechanism.
  • Joining Date and Notice Period — a stored read-only joining date, and a per-version notice period defaulted once from a company setting.
  • Multi-company by design — every setting lives on res.company, and dependants carry a company record rule.

How it works

  1. Install the module. hr and mail come along automatically, and five relationships are seeded.
  2. Open Settings → Employees → Employee Records and set the identification lead time (14 days by default), the passport lead time (180 days), the automatic employee creation switch and the default notice period (30 days).
  3. Open an employee and go to Private Information. Add dependants, tick one of them as the emergency contact — the employee's Emergency Contact and Emergency Phone update immediately, and any previously flagged dependant is unticked.
  4. Record the Identification Expiry Date and the Passport Expiration Date, and upload the scans. The two envelope buttons appear as soon as a date is filled in.
  5. Leave the rest to HR: Identity Document Expiry Warnings. Running on 06/09/2026 with the default 14-day lead time, it e-mails only the employees whose identification expires on 20/09/2026 — not the ones expiring in 45 or 365 days, and not the same person again tomorrow.

Security

No new security group is created. HR Officer (hr.group_hr_user) manages dependants, HR Administrator (hr.group_hr_manager) additionally owns the relationship master list and the notice period. Ordinary internal users can read only their own employee's dependants, enforced by a record rule rather than by hiding a field.
