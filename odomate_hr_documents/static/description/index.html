Employee Document Expiry Tracking

Work permits, licences and certificates that chase themselves

Keep one record per expiring employee document and stop discovering the expiry a week after it happened. A daily scheduled action expires what has lapsed, emails the employee, and puts a dated To-Do on their manager — following the chasing pattern you configure per document type. Renewal keeps the old dates and the old scans as history instead of overwriting them.

Key Features

Four chasing patterns

Each odomate.hr.document.type carries a pattern and a reminder delay: on the expiry date only, once N days before, every day from N days before until expiry, or every day from expiry until N days after.

Automatic expiry, then notification

One daily run: Valid documents past their date become Expired first, then every Valid or Expired document is checked against its pattern. Drafts are never touched.

Employee email + manager To-Do

Each chase sends the Employee Document: Expiry Reminder template to the employee and creates or refreshes a To-Do on employee_id.parent_id.user_id, dated on the expiry date. No manager on file means email only.

No double sends

last_reminder_sent_date stops a second automatic reminder on the same calendar day — without suppressing tomorrow's send in a daily pattern.

Renewal that preserves history

The renewal wizard copies the current dates into an odomate.hr.document.history row and re-points the existing ir.attachment records onto it — the same records, moved, not copied — then gives the document its new dates and scans.

One valid document per type

Enforced by @api.constrains on create and on write, and backed by a partial unique index so concurrent imports cannot slip a duplicate through. Draft and Expired copies may coexist.

Readable urgency

days_remaining_label reads Expired 12 days, Today, Tomorrow or a plain number, and its paired search method turns the "expiring within 7 / 30 days" filters into a plain date_expiry range — nothing stored, nothing stale.

Correct access out of the box

Employees see their own documents read-only, enforced by a record rule rather than a filtered list. HR officers manage everything; HR managers delete. Multi-company rules on documents and history.

How it works

  1. Configure the types. Employees → Employee Documents → Document Types. Set Chasing Pattern and Reminder Days for each kind of document you issue.
  2. Record a document. Employees → Employee Documents → Documents. Pick the employee and the type, enter the dates, drag the scan into the chatter. The reference EMPDOC/00001 is assigned automatically.
  3. Mark it valid. Refused if the expiry date is already in the past, and refused if the employee already holds a valid document of that type.
  4. Let the cron work. The daily scheduled action expires and chases. Expired rows turn red and bold in the list; the manager finds a dated To-Do in their activity view.
  5. Renew. Four fields — new issue date, new expiry date, new scans, reason. The old dates and scans move to the history row, the document returns to Valid, and the open To-Do is marked done.

What this module deliberately does not do

No approval workflow. No blocking of payroll, leave or scheduling. No employee self-service upload. No OCR of scans. No escalation beyond one email and one manager to-do. No printable report. The standard identification_id, passport_id and work_permit_* fields on hr.employee are left untouched.
