HR Notices, Announcements & Acknowledgements

Publish it once. Know exactly who acknowledged it.

Publish HR announcements to a precisely targeted audience, track who pressed Acknowledge, and keep HR-only saved reminders over any date field on employees, contract versions, applicants or time off. A systray counter shows every user the announcements addressed to them that they have not acknowledged yet.

Key Features

Approval workflow that cannot be skipped

Draft → Waiting for Approval → Published, with Refused and Expired as terminal branches. Publishing is limited to HR Administrators and enforced inside action_publish, not merely hidden in the view.

Precise audience targeting

Address everyone in the company, selected employees, selected departments (exact match — no hierarchy walk), or selected job positions. Someone hired after publication is still included in an "Everyone" announcement.

Acknowledgement tracking

See the acknowledged count, the audience size, and the list of people who have not acknowledged. Acknowledging is idempotent, and the acknowledgement fields are protected at field level, not just in the view.

Saved date reminders

Save the date questions HR asks every week — expiring work permits, contract versions dated this year — over hr.employee, hr.version, hr.applicant and hr.leave. Counts are evaluated as the calling user, so nobody sees records they cannot read.

Systray counter

A bullhorn in the top bar counts the published, in-window announcements addressed to you that you have not acknowledged. No polling, no websocket, and no asset loaded from any external address.

Multi-company and multilingual

Company record rules on every model, and a complete Ukrainian translation covering states, audiences, reminder windows, buttons and the systray itself.

How it works

  1. Create your categories in Employees → Configuration → Announcement Categories.
  2. Draft an announcement in Employees → Notices → All Announcements, set the display window and pick the audience. The form reveals only the target field matching your choice.
  3. Click Send for Approval. A To-Do activity is scheduled for an HR Administrator, who clicks Publish — or Refuse with a mandatory reason that lands in the chatter.
  4. Employees see the announcement under Employees → Notices → Announcements and in the systray, and press Acknowledge.
  5. HR watches the Acknowledgements tab. A daily scheduled action expires announcements once their display window closes.

Documentation

The full user guide ships with the module: doc/USER_GUIDE.md (English) and doc/USER_GUIDE.uk.md (Ukrainian). It documents the audience rules, the field reference, and the module's honest limitations — including the fact that "acknowledged" records a button press only, and is not evidence that anything was read or understood.
